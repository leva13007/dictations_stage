# Complaint about poor delivery service
Dear Sir/Madam,
I am writing to make a formal complaint about the poor delivery service I received from DPD on 25th of April 2025.
I was expecting my parcel to be delivered to my home address, but the delivery was not completed in a proper way.
According to the tracking information, the driver claimed that they had attempted delivery and provided a photograph as proof.
However, this does not show a genuine delivery attempt.
The driver only stopped their vehicle near my address, took a photograph of the house from their car, and then left without coming to the door.
Nobody from DPD rang the doorbell, knocked on the door, or contacted me by phone.
I was available to receive the parcel, but the driver did not make any real effort to deliver it.
The photograph appears to have been taken only to show the company that the driver had been at the location, even though the delivery service was not actually provided.
I find this behaviour unacceptable and very disappointing.
Customers should be able to trust that delivery drivers will make a proper attempt to deliver their parcels.
I would like DPD to investigate this incident and explain why the driver failed to complete the delivery correctly.
I also expect my parcel to be delivered as soon as possible and for steps to be taken to prevent this happening again.
I look forward to receiving your response within 14 days.
Yours faithfully,
Anna Johnson
Manager
